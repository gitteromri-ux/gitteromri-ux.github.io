Business Activities

Investment Banking

Silver Road Capital provides a comprehensive range of services in mergers and acquisitions transactions.

Placement Services

Silver Road Capital considers its Placement Services as one of the most important and growing branches of the group.

Capital Markets

Silver Road Capital is significantly involved in transactions in the capital markets, the group works in cooperation with Tier One underwriters, investment bankers and investors in order to promote creative and complex transactions in the capital markets.

Investment Funds

Silver Road Capital has developed an investment fund practice that combines the group’s knowledge, experience and extensive set of connections together with investment experts in various fields, bringing smart investments to the table.

Holdings & Investments

As part of the growth strategy of the Silver Road Capital Group, the group occasionally makes direct investments in companies and/or provides services to companies in which the group owns shares on the equity level.

Strategic Advisory for Governments

Silver Road Capital provides strategic advisory services for governments and parliaments around the world.

Consulting & Analysis

Silver Road Capital provides business financial consulting and analysis services to companies, boards of directors, management teams, investment companies, funds and governments that are interested in financial and business analysis.
