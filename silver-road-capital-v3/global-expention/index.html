About the Program

Our Israel-U.S. Business program is designed to empower Israeli companies with the tools and support needed to succeed in the U.S. market. We provide comprehensive strategic planning, helping you tailor your approach for maximum impact; facilitate valuable partnerships and network connections, opening doors to key players and decision-makers; and ensure seamless ecosystem integration, positioning your company for sustainable growth in the dynamic U.S. business landscape. This program is your gateway to unlocking unparalleled opportunities and achieving global success.

ADV Ohad Michael

Vice President and Program Manager

Ohad Michael is an accomplished business leader with extensive experience in global expansion, strategic growth, and investment management. He has successfully guided companies in entering international markets, particularly the U.S., while driving substantial growth and value creation. In senior roles such as Deputy CEO, COO, and Head of Global Markets at publicly traded and private companies, Ohad has led initiatives that optimized operations, increased revenues, and positioned businesses as market leaders.
