Investment Funds

Silver Road Capital has developed an investment fund practice that combines the group’s knowledge, experience and extensive set of connections together with investment experts in various fields, bringing smart investments to the table.

SRP1 - Silver Road Properties 1

Silver Road Properties 1 is a real estate investment fund focused on capitalizing on the burgeoning growth and untapped potential of Israel’s peripheral regions. We strategically invest across a diverse portfolio of property types, including residential, commercial, income-producing, and entrepreneurial projects, to deliver strong and consistent returns for our investors. By focusing on areas outside the central hubs, we unlock value through lower entry costs, higher growth potential, and government-backed development initiatives.

Investment Thesis:

  • Undervalued Markets: Israel’s periphery offers significant value compared to saturated central areas. Lower acquisition costs and development potential create substantial upside.
  • Government Support: The Israeli government actively promotes development in peripheral regions through incentives, infrastructure investments, and strategic planning, driving economic growth and property value appreciation.
  • Diversified Portfolio: We mitigate risk by investing across a variety of property types:
    • Residential: Addressing the growing demand for affordable housing in developing communities.
    • Commercial: Supporting the expansion of local businesses and services.
    • Income-Producing: Generating stable cash flow through established rental properties.
    • Entrepreneurial: Participating in ground-up development projects with high growth potential.
  • Economic Growth Drivers: Population growth, improved infrastructure, and expanding industries are fueling economic development in the periphery.
  • Strategic Partnerships: We leverage strong relationships with local developers, contractors, and municipalities to secure prime opportunities and ensure efficient project execution.

Germin8 Ventures

What we do?

Germin8 Ventures is a multi-stage, strategically-oriented venture capital firm investing in disruptive Food & AgTech companies across the value chain, in various stages, globally.

Who’s involved?

We’re a group of like-minded industry veterans and sophisticated investors providing investment and strategic resources to transformative Food & Agriculture Technology companies that are addressing major demand trends and improving the global food system through disruptive technologies and solutions.

What we focus on?

Our investment focus is aligned with emerging growth segments across Consumer Innovation, Industrial AgTech and FoodTech / Alternative Foods.

How we work?

The needs and work styles of entrepreneurs vary, and navigating growth to reach success is not a one-size-fits-all proposition. Simply, we aim to provide the resources and network to enable entrepreneurs to focus on what they do best.

SILVER STONE GLOBAL PARTNERS

Silver Stone Global Partners is a VC investment fund that specializes in deep technologies. The fund is managed by experts with extensive experience in the fields of blockchain, IT, software and hardware, AI, and cyber.

The co-chairman of the fund are Ambassador Danny Ayalon, former Israeli ambassador to the United States and Deputy Foreign Minister of Israel and Chairman of the Silver Road Capital Group and the distinguished Mr. Jason Greenblatt who served as the Trump administration’s Special Envoy to the Middle East and was the architect of the peace agreements between Israel to the United Arab Emirates. Mr. Greenblatt brings with him many years of experience as a senior vice president in the Trump Group as well as significant diplomatic experience and industry connections in the Gulf countries.
